# Claim and heartbeat

## Claim

Workers claim from one or more queues with an optional batch size. Claiming uses `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` over a purpose-built partial index and stamps:

- `status = running`
- `current_attempt_id`
- `lease_expires_at`
- `worker_id`

Per-resource concurrency limits (`concurrency_key`) are enforced at claim with a deadlock-free try-lock admission protocol.

## Heartbeat

Heartbeats are pure lease bumps by default (HOT updates). Progress checkpoints are batched onto heartbeats — keep `progress` small (cursor, not a result set).

On fence loss (`ok = false`) or repeated transport failures, the worker cancels the handler task immediately.
