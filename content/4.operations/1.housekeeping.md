# Housekeeping

## `taskq.tick()`

Advisory-lock-deduped maintenance (~5s):

- Reap expired leases
- Fire due schedules (client computes next fire with croniter)
- Finalize workflow rollups / dep stragglers

## Wake path

**Polling is correctness. NOTIFY is latency.**

Workers may `LISTEN` on `taskq_<queue>` for early wakeups. Missed notifications never lose jobs. Only immediately runnable inserts should notify.

## Soft stop

On SIGTERM: stop claiming → wait `soft_stop_timeout` → hard-cancel handlers → `release_job(p_cause='worker_shutdown')`.
